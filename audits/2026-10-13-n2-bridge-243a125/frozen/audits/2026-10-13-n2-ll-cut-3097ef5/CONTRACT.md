## Source, with no weakened qualification

G is a finite simple graph embedded in a disk with original ordered induced
outer cycle B=(b0,b1,b2,b3,b4). Its **complete** ordered boundary relation is
Sigma933 or Sigma941, or one common D5 image of the entire source; T4 is wholly
accepted, every original nonboundary edge is Sigma-critical, and epsilon=2.
The effective H=G-B is connected and full B-touch. Exactly two nonadjacent
original vertices r,s have complete G-degree five; every other effective
interior vertex has complete G-degree four. H-{r,s} consists exactly of two
original long mixed pieces P,Q, with no unary piece. H is biconnected; both
pieces are one-sided. Long means actual B-support is not contained in a
boundary edge. Free isolated coordinates, if any, remain independent full
four-color factors and are excluded only from the effective size count.

The entire original graph, literal vertex names and edges, contact ordering,
shared identities, owners, actual attachments, rotation, bridges and side
branches remain the source authority. Projected interface records are never
substituted for this ledger or declared graph realizations. No graph satisfying
only the three rejections below is qualified as a complete source.

The one common transported frame is fixed throughout:

```
I=(0,1,3); q0=(0,1,2,1,2); q1=(0,1,2,0,2); q3=(0,1,0,2,1); D=3.
```

No boundary rotation or color permutation is applied separately to a piece or
row. A global exchange of the named roots exchanges **all** their data in all
three rows. A global P/Q exchange merely chooses the storage order, again in
all rows simultaneously. The boundary itself is not quotiented by D5 here.

Only the upstream residual is studied: supports {3,3}, spokes (2,0) or (2,1);
supports {3,3} or {3,4}, spokes (1,1), allowing a global root exchange.
t=t_r+t_s counts original B-spokes. The already proved t=0/1 exclusions are
inputs, not new results. No minimum-interior/maximal-edge near-triangulation
degree>=5, h>=3, W5 or other research-line premise is used.

## Interface and logical boundary

For each original root v let T_v=N_B(v). For each piece C let k_C^v be its
number of original v-edges, and let W_C^v be the union of B-neighbors of the
original v-contacts. A shared contact contributes one edge to each root count.
Keep each contact's owner subset and its actual B-neighbor set separately;
W alone is a derived projection. Its row signatures are obtained by evaluating
the three literal q's on those same boundary vertex names.

For each row E_v=[4] minus q(T_v); X_C^v=q(W_C^v) intersect E_v. Basic S_C is
exactly the union of its r-collision rows, s-collision columns and (only if a
shared contact exists) its diagonal cells. No complete-coloring result adds a
basic safe cell. A separate LL-CUT+diag may add a diagonal cell only with an
identified proof of the corresponding full original fibre.

The source-to-profile map forgets internal detail for a **necessary** screen.
It does not prove the converse, planarity, criticality or Sigma. The richer
source ledger retains internal detail without a bound on block lengths;
finite contact-interface fibres retain ordered named-contact preimages.
The coverage proof and exact list are in [profiles/REPORT.md](profiles/REPORT.md).

An overlap or infeasible matrix gives a same-row pair where both complete
piece fibres are nonempty. Choosing one full tuple from each, adding exactly
that row and those ordered pins, satisfies all original edges because C's are
the original components after removing B,r,s. Include the free-coordinate
product. A feasible matrix gives none of these tuples and certifies neither
actual rejection nor a graph counterexample.
