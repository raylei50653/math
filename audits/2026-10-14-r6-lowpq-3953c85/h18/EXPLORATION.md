any deletion masks were compared. No large enumeration was run.

   common-neighbour identity made the whole collar induced at every h.

   nonnegative private-edge counts alone gave a coarse h<=15 envelope.

   p=q=5=>ell>=6 theorem. Independent geometry review checked both the
   leaf's middle-vertex fan and the empty-cycle obstruction. The final
   private graph has cycle rank at least two, giving h<=12.

claim, graph realization, new general catalogue closure, or low p/q
