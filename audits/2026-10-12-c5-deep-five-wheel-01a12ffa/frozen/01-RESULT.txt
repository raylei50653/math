2026-10-12: degree restrictions beyond the cofacial normal form

Domain: finite simple plane disk graphs with five named outer-cycle vertices.
Boundary chords are allowed. Sigma is the complete ten-row S4-orbit relation;
boundary positions are fixed and every interface uses one shared color frame.

New conditional paper conclusions:

2. The source in P2 has all boundary degrees >=4 and all interior degrees >=5.
3. It has at least six interior vertices of degree exactly five.

Proof of 1.
For a C4 source with named boundary (a,b,c,d), put a new degree-two vertex z
outside edge ab and use (a,z,b,c,d) as the outer C5, retaining ab as a chord.
The universal outside-K screen accepts all five T4 rows and therefore forbids
boundary chords in an outside-K source. This augmented source must lie in K.
The C4 orbits, in order, are ABAB, ABAC, ABCB, ABCD. Among all 16 possible C4
masks, exactly seven augment to catalogue keys:
  C4 mask:          7    10   11   12   13   14    15
  C5 key:        632   424  440  960  976 1000  1016
  witness k:       1     0    2    0    2    3     0
Every selected literal C5 witness has dummy slot 1 of degree two, adjacent
only to slots 0 and 2. Delete it to obtain the required C4 witness. c4/report.json
records literal edges, all complete fibres including empties, the entire mask
map and apex-planarity checks. All 84 C4 assignments and all 240 C5 assignments
were independently compared by direct products and MRV backtracking.

Proof of 2: boundary vertices.
In the chordless near-triangulation a boundary vertex b cannot have degree two:
its single incident inner triangle would require a boundary chord. If b has
degree three, write the outer cycle as (a,b,c,d,e) and its interior neighbor as
x. The two facial triangles abx and bcx fill the ear. Delete b and promote x
to the boundary, giving a disk R with outer cycle (a,x,c,d,e) and k(G)-1 interior
vertices. Global minimum k gives Sigma(R) in K. Replace R by its <=5-interior
witness, reattach b with edges ba,bc,bx, and return x to the interior. Complete
interface equality preserves Sigma(G), and the replacement has <=6 interiors.
P3 contradicts Sigma(G) outside K. Hence every boundary degree is >=4.

Proof of 2: interior vertices.
Degrees <=3 are removed by the standard greedy extension argument, contradicting
global minimum k. Also every occupied separating triangle can be emptied while
preserving Sigma: restrict one accepted whole-graph coloring to its inside and
permute all inside colors to match any proper coloring of its three interface
vertices. Proper triangle colorings have one S4 orbit. Thus there are no occupied
separating triangles in G; the four-neighbor link of a degree-four interior
vertex v is an induced cyclic quadrilateral (a,b,c,d).

For this link, every extendible coloring has a=c or b=d. If each opposite pair
contains at least one interior vertex, delete v and identify that pair through
the vacated disk. Both quotients preserve the five distinct boundary labels,
have fewer interior vertices and have relations in K. Their complete relations
have union Sigma(G), contradicting union closure. This proof does not require
either individual quotient to retain all rows.

Four boundary neighbors would give a C4 in the chordless boundary C5 and are
impossible. Three boundary neighbors are consecutive in the link and force
the middle boundary vertex to have degree three, already excluded. The only
remaining case has exactly two boundary neighbors, opposite in the link; they
are nonadjacent on C5. Name the outer cycle (a,b,c,d,e), and name the link
(a,x,c,y), choosing x on the short boundary arc side.

The four facial star triangles fill a-x-c-y and contain only v. Jordan
separation gives precisely a short C4 disk (a,b,c,x), a long C5 disk
(a,y,c,d,e), and this central wheel. There are no cross-cap edges. Replace
the C4 cap by its <=3-interior witness (conclusion 1), and the C5 cap by its
<=5-interior witness (global minimum k, since that cap has fewer interiors).
Use the same named colors of a,c,x,y,v when taking the complete join. Exactly
x,y,v are additional interface/interior vertices, so the reconstructed source
has at most 3+5+3=11 interiors and the same Sigma(G). P3 again contradicts
Sigma(G) outside K. Every interior degree is therefore >=5.

Proof of 3.
Euler gives E=3k+7. With D_B the sum of the five boundary degrees,
  sum_inner (6-degree) = D_B-14.
All inner degrees are >=5, all boundary degrees >=4, and therefore
  n_5 = D_B-14 + sum_inner,degree>=7 (degree-6) >=6.
