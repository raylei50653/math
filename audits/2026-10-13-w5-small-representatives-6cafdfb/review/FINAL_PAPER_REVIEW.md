## 6. Terminology clarification communicated to the parent

The user phrase “染色數相等” can denote the chromatic number chi, while
nearby discussion of fibre multiplicities concerns the number of full
colourings. It is clearer to say explicitly that neither
`chi(A)=chi(A')` nor equality of numbers of complete colourings is needed.
This is a wording clarification, not a missing hypothesis.

The existing prism family even gives a paper counterexample for chi.
A1 has the complete three-colouring

boundary-edge inequality. Every two B vertices would have different

target rows show a four-colouring, so chi(T)=4. Nonetheless the established
full capped Sigma equality holds. This clarification can be included
without a new test or a change to any immutable certificate.
