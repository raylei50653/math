# Retained non-authoritative first experiment

`EXPLORATION.json` was generated by the first version of `explore.py` and is not
a mathematical certificate. It incorrectly paired only the canonical K8 color
word with the fixed literal outer C5 word 01232. The cap's local S4 quotient
does not quotient their relative palette. Its apparent two removals must not
be interpreted as exclusions of normalized fibres.

The corrected version expands every K8 word to all distinct literal S4 images,
keeps C5 pinned to 01232, and after a swap uses the ONE palette determined by
the entire C5 word on both boundary components. All cap lifts are transported
using the same palette; no exterior or cap is normalized independently.
