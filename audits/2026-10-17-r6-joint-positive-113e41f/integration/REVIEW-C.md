# Independent reconstruction of the split-source pivotal theorem

The root reviewer reconstructed C/PROOF.md from the two frozen full-U
return rules, without using C's finite component table. The new connection
and separator claims are accepted as arbitrary-size conditional results.

In E, a separated AD component of v would produce the displayed complete
U row with r=A=z but the wrong unchanged pq palette. Thus AD connects z to
v. The actual outer order is z,s,v,w,t; the disjoint AD/BC palettes make
the z-v and s-t paths a genuine alternating-endpoint Jordan obstruction.
The retained wt edge then places w with t, away from s.

In F, a separated BC component of s would produce the old X boundary
while retaining u=D. Its complete r=D lift is legal, and every original
X lift excludes D from pq. This proves the BC s-t connection in F.
Jordan gives the opposite AD separation. Both arguments apply to every
full colouring; private vertices are transported with their components.

For the CD component K of u separated from m, splitting m into s,t
does not change K: a component disjoint from m contains neither split
copy and gains no new incidence. Switching K fixes all the outer pins.
An old AD path can disappear only by losing an old D vertex of K; a new
BC path can appear only by using such a vertex, now C. These elementary
induced-subgraph facts prove both mandatory-intersection conclusions.
They hold independently for every background orientation of the other
actual CD components. The component containing m stays fixed, so each
background has the same E/F row types and the same legal r=D lift.

The F-to-111 bridge is also exact. The retained zu edge puts z and u in
one AD component, distinct from that of v. Switching the whole latter
component fixes the other displayed pins and changes v=A to D. The same
component gives the inverse on the stated H subfibre. In any H row,
the retained mv edge puts m and v together in CD. If u were separate,
its whole CD switch would leave v fixed and violate FJ with its explicit
r=A lift. Therefore m,u connect after the ordered two exchanges. Any
new CD path must use a newly admitted old A vertex of the second component.

The third ordered p/q transport uses an actual pq BC component separated
from the mw component; it swaps both actual endpoint colours and has an
explicit inverse. It does not require a p/q graph automorphism or reflect
the original support mask. An existing mp or mq edge precludes the
specified separation directly.

The constructive two-exchange exit is an exit from the **original all-r
source rule**. Its last U row has s!=t; it is not itself a constructed
forbidden J colouring or a positive-weight matching. The proof explicitly
keeps this distinction. It does not show that a source supplies an
avoiding path: the new theorem instead forces a separator whenever the
initial CD separation occurs. No joint positivity, strict oddness
decrease, or completed-source descent is accepted.
