# Independent reconstruction of the seven-defect lemma

The root reviewer read A/PROOF.md independently of its checker. This
review accepts A1--A3 as source-conditional paper results. It does not
accept an even-complement existence or source exclusion conclusion.

1. In the actual cubic dual, a complementary cycle uses two incidences at
   every vertex, so each unused incidence is a matching edge. Source
   minimum degree four and faciality of all primal triangles give a
   triangle-free, three-edge-connected dual with only trivial three-cuts.
2. A dual four- or five-cycle cannot have a chord, and cannot have vertices
   on both sides because each nonempty side needs at least three outgoing
   edges. It is therefore facial. A chordless nonfacial seven-component
   would have a side attached by exactly three matching spokes. Triviality
   of that cut forces one outside vertex, which cannot receive three
   matching edges. This supplies the step specific to a complementary
   component; an arbitrary seven-cycle has no such matching argument.
3. A chord of a seven-component belongs to its matching and splits the
   cycle into lengths four and five. Both are faces on the chord side.
   Two chords on different sides would exhaust the whole cubic graph on
   seven vertices, violating even order. Exactly one chord remains.
4. The five other unused incidences have distinct outer endpoints and form
   an actual cyclic five-cut. Each other dual vertex belongs to another
   whole complementary cycle. Three-edge connectivity makes both sides
   of this five-cut connected, so the primal image is a simple C5.
5. A chord of that C5 creates a primal triangle, hence an actual dual
   vertex incident to two cut edges in the matching, impossible. The
   two-star side has exactly two primal vertices. If the other side had
   one vertex, J would have eight vertices, while the six neighbours and
   two distinct nonneighbours of z already force at least nine. The old
   source theorem therefore places this same induced C5 in its crossed
   sector cases. No witnesses from different matchings are combined.
6. The marked alternatives follow from the actual endpoints of g=mz and
   f=pq, the degree bounds, and the four old p/q-to-v/w nonedges. In
   particular a nonfacial seven-component containing f cannot have an
   unmarked degree-four centre: every possible degree-four centre other
   than its incident terminal is excluded by these incidences.

The lemma leaves remote odd components, facial seven-components, crossed
five-cut configurations and odd components of length at least nine. It
does not assert that the omitted active mark lies on an odd component.
That retained possibility is essential to the joint-minimum stopping
point. Finite controls can calibrate A1 and its shape data; the old source
sector theorem supplies the arbitrary-size source part of A2.
