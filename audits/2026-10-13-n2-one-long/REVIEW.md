   connected and does not remove any attachment outside h. In the boundary-arc

   the other arc's interior belongs to the remaining-L bag. The latter is

   disjointness/connectedness claims follow from original edges, for arbitrary
   cycle and chain length. If a=h, a remaining original contact supplies the
   bag-to-bag edge; kL>=4 and no shared contacts ensure it exists.

   connected. Long support supplies an original attachment outside ab; the
   two endpoint-to-root/short adjacencies are original spokes, not inferred

   exact owner counts kLr=kLs=2 and kSr=kSs=1. No cutoff on internal blocks is
   introduced, and four/five-point interfaces have not been compressed.

   at least kSs+kLs-1 colors and only kSs internal neighbors. With kLs>=2,
   strict slack colors every original exterior vertex. Fix that actual full
   exterior tuple, including its actual s color, before coloring L by the
   collision slack. The resulting contradiction is a whole original G lift
   in one rejected row. Root labels and ownership are never normalized away.
